Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Aure iOS app ("Aure", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Aure is built so that your photos and results stay on your device. We do not run our own servers, we do not require an account, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

Aure is published by Furkan Erdemir. You can contact us at furkanerdemir1925@gmail.com.

2. Photos and selfie analysis

  • When you take a selfie or choose a photo, Aure analyzes it on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework to detect facial landmarks.
  • Your photos, facial landmark data and analysis results are not uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • We do not use your photos or facial data to identify you, for advertising, or to train any model.
  • The app only accesses your camera or photo library after you grant permission, and only for the photo you choose. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, Aure stores the following locally in the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • the photo you selected for analysis and your analysis results,
  • progress entries you create over time,
  • your preferences, such as language and onboarding status.

This data never leaves your device through Aure unless you choose to share it (see section 5). It may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time using the reset option in the app or by deleting the app.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Aure offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment card details.

We use Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.) to manage subscriptions. To verify purchases and determine whether you have premium access, Adapty receives purchase and subscription information from the App Store together with an anonymous app-generated profile ID and basic technical device information (such as device model, OS version, app version, locale and IP address). Advertising identifiers (IDFA) are not collected. Adapty processes this information on our behalf under its own privacy policy: adapty.io/privacy.

5. Sharing your results

If you use the share feature, the content you choose is passed to the iOS share sheet and sent to the app or person you select. That recipient's handling of the content is governed by their own terms and policies.

6. Content loaded from the internet

Some exercise illustrations are loaded from a public GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com). When these images load, GitHub receives standard technical request data such as your IP address. No personal data from Aure is included in these requests.

7. What we do not do

  • No account or login is required.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you reset the app's data or delete the app. Subscription records held by Apple and Adapty are retained as required to provide the service and meet legal obligations. To request deletion of subscription-related data held by Adapty on our behalf, contact us at the email above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because most data stays only on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the app. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

10. Children

Aure is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Health disclaimer

Aure's scores and suggestions are estimates for entertainment and general self-improvement purposes only. They are not medical, psychological or scientific assessments.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be reflected in the app or on this page.
